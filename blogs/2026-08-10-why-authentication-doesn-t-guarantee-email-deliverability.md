One of the most common misconceptions I encounter with email marketers is the belief that email authentication guarantees good deliverability. Their logic seems reasonable. If SPF, DKIM, and DMARC are configured correctly, mailbox providers should trust the sender and deliver messages to the subscriber’s inbox.
